The team

Three people, one design function

We work across the whole platform — shipments, bookings, settings, the screens people have open all day. Here is who does what, and what they have been on.

We are a small team embedded across product, not a service desk you raise a ticket with. The earlier you pull us in the more useful we are — ideally while the problem is still being argued about, not once the solution has been decided and needs decorating.

If you want us on something, talk to Craig. If you want to know what we have been up to, that is what the rest of this site is for.

  • Craig Wetherall

    Product Design Lead

    Runs the design function and does the least glamorous half of the job — sequencing, saying no, and making sure the thing we shipped is the thing we argued for. Has opinions about tables that he will share unprompted.
