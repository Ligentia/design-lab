Reading 200 rows without losing your place

Twenty explorations of the shipment table later, we landed on a frozen spine and a density ladder.

Borderless and rule-separated in editorial; bordered, padded and raised in dark. Same markup.

Prose

The ops floor does not read a table, it scans one. Any given controller is looking for the four shipments that changed since yesterday, and everything else on screen is weather. That reframing is what unlocked the work.

What we changed

We froze the identifying columns into a spine that never scrolls away, then built a three-step density ladder so the same table can be comfortable, compact or micro without becoming a different component.

  • A frozen spine carrying reference, client and status
  • Row heights at 36, 28 and 20 pixels
  • Tabular figures everywhere a number can change
